min(3.4GiB ceiling, 0.60*MemAvailable, MemAvailable-2.25GiB reserve) --
unified-DRAM guard so patrol can't push Linux into reclaim/OOM that would
masquerade as SEEs (65bdcf5 + config). On board 01 the reserve bound binds:
3303 MB allocated = 43.4% of DRAM, MemAvailable 3.2 GiB during workload.
1-h endurance validation running (run 20260814-170112_endurance-mem34_3600s,
memlog unit endurance-memlog). Acceptance: MemAvailable >= 1.5 GiB hard /
2 GiB preferred, zero swap growth, no OOM in dmesg, no PSI stalls, no service
restarts. If it fails, fall back to auto_fraction 0.5 flat (~2.8 GB).
SEL CAVEAT (same review): a warm reboot does NOT clear a true latch-up --
only a power removal does. automatic_reboot stays DISABLED; beam-day SEL
response = operator/external power cut (facility-level item; the 5 s
over-limit dwell should also be sanity-checked against facility guidance).
Cross-section bookkeeping: use the LOGGED buffer_mb (actual allocation) as
the monitored-bits denominator, never the requested fraction.
